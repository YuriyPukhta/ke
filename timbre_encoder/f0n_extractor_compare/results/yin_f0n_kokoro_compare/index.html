Audio is synthesized with official Kokoro voices. Internal curves come from F0Ntrain. Library curves from YIN (Hz) + STFT log-energy on the same generated wav.

  • raw — resized only (Hz / log-E as-is)

Note: Kokoro F0 is ~Hz on voiced frames but ≈0 (sometimes slightly negative) on UV. YIN invents pitch on silence → full-curve correlation looks worse than voiced-only.
